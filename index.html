Welcome to Algo Pals!

The main premise of the game is to keep your pet happy and avoid letting them run away!


How do you keep your pet happy, you may ask?

There are four (4) ways to interact with your pet to achieve this: Feeding, Playing, Grooming & Resting.

Each positive interaction with items add to the individual meters. Dont let the meters go to 0%! You will earn "Algo-Points" which can be used to get extra goodies for your pet!

Best of luck and have fun!
